Privacy Policy

Swaya is designed to keep your personal media library private on your own Windows PC.

1. Desktop Application (Swaya for Windows)

Your local video collection, photo galleries, favorite lists, finish history, and personal stats stay directly on your Windows PC. Swaya does not upload or sell your local media files. Online requests occur only when you use connected features, such as license activation, app updates, streaming trailers, fetching online catalog images, or the Friends feature.

2. License Activation & Billing

Memberships and Founding Lifetime Passes are processed by Patreon. License verification exchanges only the minimum licensing data needed to validate your active key or membership tier.

3. This Website (swaya.xyz)

swaya.xyz is a static website with no analytics trackers or advertising cookies. Selecting a language stores only your chosen locale code in browser localStorage (swaya_web_locale) so the switcher remembers your preference.

4. Contact

For privacy questions or support requests, email support@swaya.xyz or join our Discord community.
