Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, simple answers on privacy, supported videos and photos, PC requirements, and licensing.

Do I need an internet connection to use Swaya?

Your videos, photo galleries, favorite lists, finish history, and personal stats all stay private on your Windows PC and work offline. You only need internet to activate your license, get updates, stream trailers, load online cover images, or use the Friends feature.

How is Swaya different from browser tools like Stash?

Swaya is a real Windows desktop app: just install and open it, with no local server setup or browser window needed. It comes with a ready-to-use catalog, automatic scene matching with 1-click undo, a floating mini-player (PiP), photo galleries, instant F10 hide mode, and much more.

How do Patreon membership and the Founding Lifetime Pass work?

Swaya is a paid Windows app. Joining on Patreon (Member €8/mo, VIP €15/mo, or Executive €25/mo) unlocks full access, updates, and up to 3 PCs. The Founding Lifetime Pass (€97 once, limited to 50) gives you lifetime access and updates on up to 3 PCs, with your license key sent via Patreon DM shortly after purchase (usually right away, within a few hours at most).

How do the F10 hide mode and 'B' blur work?

Pressing F10 instantly mutes sound and switches to a clean decoy screen. Pressing 'B' adds a soft blur while you browse.

Which video and photo file formats and codecs does Swaya support?

Swaya plays virtually all common video formats natively, including MP4, MKV, WebM, MOV, AVI, WMV, FLV, and M4V, with hardware-accelerated decoding for H.264, HEVC (H.265), AV1, and VP9 up to 4K/60fps, plus a floating Picture-in-Picture (PiP) mini-player. For photo galleries, Swaya supports JPG/JPEG, PNG, WebP, GIF, and AVIF.

How does automatic file organization work, and is 1-click undo safe for my files?

When you drop a folder of messy filenames into the Organizer, Swaya shows a full preview of matched titles, stars, studios, and covers before changing anything on your drive. Applying a batch renames and sorts files cleanly, and every batch is logged so you can revert your files back to their exact original names and folders in 1 click.

Which websites work with the built-in Link Importer?

The built-in Link Grabber imports direct videos, streams, and multi-page photo/video galleries from Reddit, RedGIFs, Coomer, Bunkr, Lmlib, and 25+ supported sites directly into your local library, complete with automatic duplicate detection and gallery merging.

What types of content does the catalog recognize (Studios, OnlyFans, JAV, PMVs)?

Swaya covers mainstream adult studios, independent/OnlyFans creators, JAV releases (by code or performer), and PMVs. Each star profile connects aliases, co-stars, studio appearances, and full filmographies. You can also add custom tags, covers, and notes to any personal or unmatched clip.

How do PIN lock, auto-camouflage, and the optional Friends mode protect my privacy?

Alongside the F10 instant mute & decoy screen and the B quick blur key, you can lock Swaya with a 4–6 digit PIN and enable automatic camouflage when you step away from your desk. Friends mode is turned off by default, has no public directory, works only via private Friend Code, and never uploads your local videos or watch history.

Will Swaya run smoothly on my Windows PC?

Yes, if you have 64-bit Windows 10 or 11, a dual-core processor, 4 GB RAM, and 20 GB free disk space. For large libraries and 4K HEVC playback, a modern quad-core CPU, 8 GB+ RAM, and an SSD are recommended. Swaya is built exclusively for Windows 10/11 (no macOS, Linux, or mobile app).

How many PCs can I use with one license, and can I move it to a new computer?

Every Patreon plan (Member, VIP, Executive) and the Founding Lifetime Pass lets you activate Swaya on up to 3 of your own Windows PCs at the same time, and you can manage or transfer activations if you upgrade your hardware.

How discreet is Patreon billing, and how do updates or cancellations work?

All charges are processed by Patreon, so your bank statement only shows Patreon, never the app name or adult keywords. Monthly plans can be canceled anytime on Patreon in 1 click with no lock-in, and new Windows app updates are delivered straight through the built-in updater.
